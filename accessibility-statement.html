FCV Restoration & Construction is committed to making fcvrestoration.com usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnification, voice control, or keyboard-only navigation.

01

Our Commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines are the internationally recognised standard for making web content more accessible to people with visual, hearing, motor, and cognitive disabilities.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort rather than a one-time project. As we update the Site, we work to maintain and improve its accessibility.

02

Measures We Take

Steps we take to keep the Site accessible include:

  • Providing text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Maintaining sufficient colour contrast between text and its background
  • Ensuring the Site can be navigated using a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator
  • Using clear heading structure and descriptive link text
  • Designing forms with associated labels and clear error messaging
  • Building layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and support browser zoom
  • Respecting reduced-motion preferences set at the operating system level
03

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some limitations may remain. In particular:

  • Some third-party content embedded in the Site — such as maps, review widgets, or video players — is outside our direct control and may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Older project photographs may have limited alternative text where the original context was not recorded.

Where we identify a barrier, we work to correct it or to provide the same information through an accessible alternative.

04

Feedback and Assistance

If you encounter a barrier on this Site, or need information from it provided in a different format, please tell us. Where possible, include the page address, a description of the problem, and the browser and assistive technology you were using — this helps us reproduce and fix the issue.

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within five business days. If you need information from the Site urgently, please call us and we will provide it directly.

Alternative Ways to Reach Us

Every service on this Site is also available by phone or email. You do not need to use a web form to request an estimate, schedule an inspection, or ask a question about a project — call or email us and we will handle it directly.

06
