This Cookie Policy explains how FCV Restoration & Construction ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on the website located at fcvrestoration.com (the "Site"). It supplements our Privacy Policy, which describes how we handle personal information more generally.

01

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is being used.

We also use similar technologies, including web beacons (small transparent images used to measure whether a page or email was opened) and pixels (snippets of code that record activity for analytics or advertising).

Cookies may be session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain until they expire or you delete them. They may be set by us ("first-party") or by a third-party service we use.

02

Categories of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary

Required for the Site to function — for example, maintaining your session, remembering form input as you move between steps, and supporting security. These cannot be switched off through our Site.

Performance and analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the Site — which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, where they arrive from, and where they leave. We use this to improve the Site's structure and content. Our Privacy Policy identifies Google Analytics as a service we may use for this purpose.

Functional

Remember choices you make, such as region or display preferences, so you do not have to re-enter them. Disabling these may reduce the convenience of the Site but will not prevent you from using it.

Marketing and advertising

Used to measure the effectiveness of our marketing and, in some cases, to show our advertising to people who have visited the Site. Our Privacy Policy identifies the Meta/Facebook Pixel as a service we may use for this purpose.

03

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on the Site are set by third parties that provide services to us. These providers may collect information about your activity on our Site and across other sites, in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Third-party services that may set cookies include analytics providers, advertising and social media platforms, embedded content such as maps or video players, and review platforms.

04

How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. You can usually find these controls under a "Privacy," "Security," or "Cookies and site data" menu. Common browsers include Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge — each publishes its own instructions in its help documentation.

You can also:

  • Use your browser's private or incognito mode, which discards cookies at the end of the session
  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the browser add-on published by Google
  • Adjust ad personalisation settings directly within the advertising platforms you use
  • Enable a "Do Not Track" or global privacy control signal, where your browser supports it

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect how parts of the Site work — for example, forms may not retain your input.
